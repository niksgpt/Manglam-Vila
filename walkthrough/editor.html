Opened from http://localhost:8765/editor.html, every change is written straight into the plan file js/plan-data.js, and the 3D walkthrough uses it. Each floor has its own plan file; switch floors with the tabs at the top. Opened any other way, changes stay in this browser only; download the plan file to keep them.

How to

Drag an item to move it. Drag the round handles to resize. Terraces, balconies and the double-height opening are resized by their handles or by typing sizes; their floor and railings follow. Type exact sizes on the right as 12'-6", 12.5 or 150". Delete removes the selected item. Drag empty paper to pan; scroll to zoom. Arrow keys nudge by one snap step.

X is measured north to south, Y from the rear wall toward the road. Floors, furniture and the guided tour path do not move with the walls; they need refitting after a layout change.
